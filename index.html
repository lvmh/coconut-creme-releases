what you need
you'll need an IPTV subscription.

coconut creme is a player — it doesn't come with content. you need a subscription from a provider that supports Xtream Codes or M3U playlists.

your provider gives you a server URL, username, and password. enter those on the login screen.

search "xtream codes IPTV" to find providers — most offer a trial before you commit.

install
01
download the .dmg
click the chip above and open the disk image
02
drag to applications
move coconut creme.app into your applications folder
03
remove quarantine flag
macOS blocks apps distributed outside the app store — run this once in terminal:
$ xattr -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/coconut creme.app"
if macOS still blocks it, go to system settings → privacy & security and click open anyway
04
launch & connect
open the app, pick holiday or cow, and enter your IPTV credentials
